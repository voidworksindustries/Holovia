1. Introduction and Scope

Voidworks Industries operates the mobile game Holovia, its online game features, Holovia challenge-sharing pages, and the Voidworks website. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, when it is shared, how long it may be kept, and the choices and rights available to users.

Voidworks Industries determines the purposes and means of processing for data used to operate Holovia and is the data controller for that processing. Some advertising, analytics, app-store, and platform providers may also act as independent controllers for data they process under their own policies.

Using Holovia does not by itself constitute consent to every type of data processing. Holovia requests an age category first, then asks for optional in-app privacy choices where applicable. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) handles advertising consent, notification permission is requested by the operating system only after you enable notifications, and Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) is shown separately on iOS after the relevant 16+ choices. Other processing is carried out to provide requested features, protect the Service, complete purchases, or pursue the purposes and legal bases described below.

2. Important Summary

  • Players select “Under 16” or “16 or older.” Under-16 mode is offline-only.
  • Under-16 and offline mode do not create a Firebase account, upload cloud data, start optional SDKs, or use online services.
  • In 16+ online mode, a pseudonymous Firebase account and related identifiers may be created to support online features.
  • Player-chosen display names, scores, and some challenge or lobby information may be visible to other players.
  • Multiplayer, spectating, challenge, leaderboard, replay, notification, analytics, and diagnostic data may be transmitted to cloud services.
  • Core progression such as coins, owned items, settings, and entitlements is primarily stored locally, but related scores, statistics, and online-feature data may also be stored online.
  • Holovia uses advertising and attribution services, including Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, Google AdMob, and Tenjin.
  • Voidworks Industries does not directly receive or store full payment-card information.
  • Voidworks Industries does not sell personal data for money.

3. Information You Provide

Player Name

Holovia asks you to choose a player display name. The game may add a suffix or generate a related unique player name so players can be distinguished. The name is stored locally and may be uploaded to Firebase for leaderboards, challenges, multiplayer, notifications, and other online features. It may be shown publicly or to opponents and spectators.

Do not use your real name, email address, phone number, home address, school, or other identifying or sensitive information as your player name.

Age Category and Advertising Choice

Holovia asks whether the user is “Under 16” or “16 or older.” Holovia stores only the selected age category locally and does not request or store a date of birth. Under-16 mode is offline-only and blocks multiplayer, challenges, spectating, leaderboards, online replays, score uploads, and other online features. Existing players may see the updated age request again when the privacy-state version changes.

Optional Privacy Choices

Where applicable, Holovia separately asks whether you allow “Help Improve Holovia” data and whether you allow “Share Crash Reports.” These choices are stored locally and can be reopened in Settings. Refused optional events are not queued for later transmission.

Notification Preferences

If you enable notifications, Holovia processes your notification settings and may store related information such as language, platform, app version, daily-mission status, and streak timing. Holovia does not request notification permission or upload an FCM token at startup. A token is used only after notification permission and the relevant in-game choice allow notifications. When notifications are disabled, Holovia deletes the local and server-side FCM token where available.

Support Communications

If you contact Voidworks Industries, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, player information, message, and any attached diagnostic material. Do not send information that is not needed to resolve your request.

4. Identifiers and Technical Information

Holovia and its service providers may process:

  • A locally generated player UUID
  • An anonymous Firebase Authentication user ID
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging registration tokens, only when notifications are enabled
  • Tenjin installation identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers, such as Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, when available and permitted
  • Other platform or device identifiers, such as an app-set or vendor identifier
  • IP address and an approximate country or region inferred from technical information
  • Device model, operating system, platform, language, locale, and time zone
  • App version, build information, package identifier, and SDK version
  • Network, tracking-permission, and limited-ad-tracking status
  • Apple Game Center account information made available by Apple, such as the authenticated Game Center user name

Holovia does not request precise GPS location for these features. Advertising and service providers may derive an approximate location from an IP address or device settings.

5. Gameplay, Analytics, and Diagnostic Information

Information processed in connection with gameplay may include:

  • Game mode, games played, session and run duration
  • Scores, high scores, coins collected, and challenge or multiplayer results
  • In-game grid position, movement, actions, dashes, flow state, wall state, and death context
  • Performance information such as target, average, minimum, and maximum frame rate, render scale, low-frame-rate time, and hitch count
  • Pause and quit information
  • App opens, installs, attribution source, sessions, and interactions
  • Crash reports, stack traces, startup logs, device state, app state, and related diagnostics

Firebase Analytics is optional and is started only after you allow “Help Improve Holovia.” Holovia does not queue analytics events after you refuse or withdraw that choice. Firebase Crashlytics is started only after you allow “Share Crash Reports” and is used to diagnose crashes, startup failures, and stability problems. Advertising consent, ATT, and notification permission are separate choices and do not automatically grant these optional analytics or crash-reporting choices.

6. Local Save Data and Cloud-Connected Data

Core save data is primarily stored locally on the device. This may include coins, currency, skins, locker items, settings, preferences, missions, streaks, purchase entitlements, and general progress.

Some related information is transmitted to online services only when an online feature is available and used. This includes player names, identifiers, scores, games played, last-online timestamps, challenge and multiplayer information, notification data, analytics events, diagnostics, and race replays. Therefore, not all gameplay-related information is stored only on the device.

Uninstalling Holovia, clearing app data, or resetting a device may permanently remove local save data. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete data already stored in Firebase, analytics systems, advertising systems, platform services, or app-store records.

7. Online Features and Visibility to Other Players

Leaderboards

Leaderboards may display your player name, unique player name, score, and rank. Associated records may include your anonymous Firebase user ID, games played, app version, platform, and timestamps.

Challenges and Race Replays

Creating or joining a challenge may upload the creator and participant identifiers, player names, scores, mode, status, timestamps, reward or entry values, and a compressed race replay. A replay can contain a detailed timeline of in-game movement, actions, world state, and visual information needed to reproduce the run.

Public challenges and their player names, scores, and summaries may be visible to other users. Challenge codes can be shared through web links.

Multiplayer and Spectating

Multiplayer and spectating use cloud services to exchange lobby identifiers, player identifiers and names, readiness, connection presence, last-seen timestamps, live position, score, actions, wall and world state, selected visual or skin data, game-over state, results, and disconnect or timeout reports. This information is shared with the relevant opponent, spectator, and backend systems as required to provide the feature.

8. Firebase and Google Cloud Services

Holovia uses the following Firebase services provided by Google when required by the selected mode and feature:

  • Firebase Authentication: creates and maintains a pseudonymous anonymous account and user ID for 16+ online features. Holovia does not use Firebase email/password login, and does not create this account in Under-16 or offline mode.
  • Cloud Firestore: stores user profiles, display names, notification settings and tokens, leaderboards, statistics, challenges, lobbies, operation requests, results, ranks, and related metadata.
  • Firebase Realtime Database: synchronizes live multiplayer, spectating, presence, movement, action, world-state, disconnect, and version-status data.
  • Cloud Storage for Firebase: stores compressed challenge race-replay files in paths associated with a Firebase user ID.
  • Cloud Functions for Firebase: performs backend processing for multiplayer, results, leaderboards, notifications, rate limits, fraud prevention, version checks, and scheduled cleanup.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: provides remote push notifications and issues a device/app registration token.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: records app, gameplay, device, and performance events only after “Help Improve Holovia” is allowed.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: collects crash and diagnostic information only after “Share Crash Reports” is allowed.
  • Firebase Hosting: hosts Holovia challenge-sharing and app-link pages.

For customer data stored in Firebase backend services, Voidworks Industries generally acts as controller and Google generally acts as processor. Google may also process service data under its own terms and privacy policy.

More information is available from Firebase Privacy and Security, Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms, and the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Advertising and Attribution

Holovia displays rewarded, interstitial, and banner advertisements. The current advertising and attribution integration includes:

  • Unity LevelPlay mediation, including ironSource advertising services
  • Unity Ads
  • Google AdMob
  • Tenjin mobile attribution

These services may process advertising or vendor identifiers, IP address, approximate country, device and app information, ad interactions, impressions, clicks, install source, app opens, tracking status, and fraud-prevention signals. Mediation providers may make an ad request available to advertising demand partners in order to fill it.

In regions where consent is required, Google UMP displays and records the advertising consent choices. Holovia requests ads and initializes LevelPlay only after UMP allows ad requests. The UMP message covers the configured mediation partners, including LevelPlay/ ironSource, Unity Ads, and Google AdMob. If personalized advertising is declined, ads may still be shown where permitted, and limited information may still be processed to deliver contextual ads, limit repetition, provide rewards, maintain security, and prevent fraud.

On iOS, Holovia shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt only after the relevant 16+ privacy choices and before requesting tracking access where the system requires it. Under-16 and offline mode do not start ATT or advertising SDKs. Denying tracking permission limits access to the IDFA but does not necessarily stop all contextual advertising or processing based on non-advertising identifiers that is otherwise permitted.

Tenjin is connected only when advertising measurement is permitted. It may then be used to measure installs, app opens, and advertising attribution. Depending on device and permission settings, Tenjin may process an installation ID, advertising or vendor ID, IP address, app and SDK version, operating system, device model, locale, country, and tracking status. Tenjin is not started and attribution events are not sent in Under-16 or offline mode or after advertising measurement permission is refused.

See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, Unity Privacy Policy, Google Advertising Policies, and the Tenjin Privacy Policy.

10. Notifications

Holovia can schedule local notifications on the device and can use Firebase Cloud Messaging for remote notifications after you enable them. Remote notifications may concern expiring streaks, daily missions, leaderboard changes, challenges, multiplayer, or important app updates.

Remote notifications require a Firebase Cloud Messaging token. The token and related metadata, such as platform, app version, update time, notification settings, language, and relevant streak or mission status, may be stored in Firestore. You can disable notification permission in your device settings or turn notifications off in Holovia. Disabling notifications removes the local token and requests deletion of the server-side token where available.

11. Platform Services and In-App Purchases

Holovia uses Unity In-App Purchasing to communicate with the Google Play Store and Apple App Store. Purchase options may include the Remove Ads Package and Supporter Package. Product identifiers, prices, order status, purchase status, and entitlement information may be processed by Unity and the relevant app store. In offline or Under-16 mode, online purchases are unavailable and Holovia must not initialize purchase or cloud services that are not needed for offline play.

Payments are completed by Google Play or Apple. Voidworks Industries does not directly process or store full credit-card or bank-account details. Local entitlement records are used to apply purchased benefits, and store records may be checked to restore or reconcile entitlements after a reinstall or device change.

On iOS, Apple Game Center may be used for authentication, displaying Game Center features, submitting high scores, and restoring high scores when that platform feature is enabled and allowed. It is not used in Under-16 or offline mode. Apple may provide Holovia with a Game Center user name and score information. Apple's processing is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

12. Website and Shared Links

The main Voidworks website is delivered through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web-request information such as IP address, browser or device information, requested URL, date and time, and security logs. Review the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Holovia challenge links and app-link landing pages may be delivered through Firebase Hosting. Opening a shared link may reveal the challenge code in the URL and may produce ordinary hosting request logs. If a link opens the Holovia app, the app may then request the associated challenge data from Firebase.

If you contact Voidworks Industries by email, your email provider and our email provider process the message and related delivery information under their own terms.

16. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including service operation, security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, and enforcement. Current operational retention includes:

  • Local save data remains until it is overwritten, reset, cleared, or removed with the app.
  • Multiplayer operation requests are generally configured to expire after approximately 24 hours.
  • Live multiplayer lobbies and sessions are generally configured to expire after approximately 30 minutes, subject to asynchronous cleanup.
  • Multiplayer result records are generally configured to expire after approximately 7 days.
  • Challenge lobby records are generally configured to expire after approximately 7 days, subject to asynchronous cleanup.
  • Pending notification-queue records may be kept for up to approximately 30 days; handled queue records are generally scheduled to expire sooner, commonly after approximately 3 days.
  • Challenge replay files may remain until they are no longer needed for the challenge, integrity checks, dispute handling, maintenance cleanup, or a verified deletion request.
  • User profile, notification-token, leaderboard, rank, and aggregated game-statistic records may remain while the online service operates or until deletion is requested and legally permitted.
  • Support messages may be retained while needed to resolve the request and maintain reasonable business, safety, or legal records.
  • Analytics, crash, attribution, advertising, app-store, and platform information is retained according to configured provider settings and the providers' applicable retention rules.

Deletion and expiration jobs are asynchronous, so data may remain for a limited period after its expiration time. Security logs, fraud-prevention records, legal records, and backups may be kept longer where reasonably necessary or legally required.

17. International Data Transfers

Voidworks Industries and its providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States and other countries where Google, Unity, Tenjin, Apple, GitHub, or their subprocessors operate. Data-protection laws in those countries may differ.

Where required, transfers are supported by measures such as adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable, contractual protections, and provider security measures. Firebase Authentication is operated from United States data centers, while other Firebase services may use global infrastructure or selected cloud regions.

18. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. These include authenticated access, Firebase security rules, restricted backend permissions, rate limits, validation, encrypted network transport, and provider security controls. Firebase services generally encrypt supported customer data in transit and at rest according to the service's documentation.

No transmission or storage system is completely secure. Voidworks Industries cannot guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid placing identifying or sensitive information in public player names or shared challenge content.

20. Children and Younger Users

Holovia is a general-audience game and is not primarily directed to children. The app asks for an age category of “Under 16” or “16 or older.” When a user selects “Under 16,” Holovia keeps the player in offline-only mode, does not create a Firebase account, does not upload cloud data, and does not start Analytics, Tenjin, push, ATT, advertising, or other optional online SDKs. Under-16 players do not use multiplayer, challenges, spectating, leaderboards, online replays, or online score submission.

Holovia does not ask for a date of birth, real name, postal address, or direct contact details during normal gameplay. It does require a player-chosen display name and uses pseudonymous identifiers and limited operational data for online features. Children must not use a real name or other identifying information as their player name.

A user who is below the age at which they can lawfully make privacy choices in their country should use Holovia only with the involvement and authorization of a parent or legal guardian where required. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information or who want to review or delete a child's information should contact Flint.tilley@voidworks-Industries.com.
